Blue's Picks · Laguna Beach

The dog-friendly guide

Laguna Beach is one of the most dog-friendly cities in California. Blue has sniffed out the best spots — from off-leash beaches to dog-welcoming patios.

Dog-Friendly Beaches

Laguna Beach has dedicated dog-friendly stretches. Blue's favorite is the off-leash area at the north end — wide open, great waves to chase.

Outdoors

Dog-Welcoming Patios

Half the restaurants in Laguna welcome dogs on their patios. Blue's picks include oceanfront spots where the vibe is as good as the food.
